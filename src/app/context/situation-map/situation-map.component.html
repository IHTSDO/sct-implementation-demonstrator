Context Representation Transformations

Transform SNOMED CT situations with explicit context into their FHIR resource representation.

This demo reads the normal form of a situation with explicit context — a finding or a procedure — detects its context attributes (finding/procedure context, subject relationship and temporal context) and applies the transformation decision table to produce the matching FHIR resource.
